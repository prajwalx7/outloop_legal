2. Description of Service

Hublu is a digital wellbeing utility and specialized sandboxed browser engineered to help users reclaim their attention and access social networks intentionally. The application provides two interconnected focus tools:

  • Intentional Webview Browser: Utilizes client-side style rules and local DOM filtering scripts solely within the user's isolated webview session to hide addictive short-form video shelves (such as YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels) while preserving direct messaging, search, and purposeful navigation.
  • Native App Blocker: An optional system-level protection feature powered by the Android AccessibilityService API (and Apple Screen Time on iOS). When enabled, Hublu intercepts the launch of designated native social media apps installed on your device and displays a focus shield barrier to prevent impulsive doomscrolling.
