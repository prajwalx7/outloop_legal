Account & Data Deletion

Compliance with Google Play & App Store Standards • Effective: October 2026

1. Overview & Immediate In-App Deletion

In accordance with user privacy rights and Google Play & Apple App Store data safety regulations, Hublu provides an instantaneous, self-serve mechanism to permanently delete your account and all associated data.

To delete your account immediately inside the app:

  1. Navigate to the Profile tab in Hublu.
  2. Tap Account Settings (the first category card).
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Tap Delete Account, enter your credentials if prompted to verify ownership, and confirm the permanent deletion.

2. What Data Is Permanently Purged

Upon triggering account deletion, the following data is irrevocably deleted from both our cloud database (Cloud Firestore & Firebase Auth) and your local device:

  • Authentication Records: Your Firebase Authentication user account, unique UID, authentication tokens, email address, display name, and avatar are immediately purged.
  • Onboarding & Baseline Audit Data: Your Day-1 onboarding responses, calculated scroll hours lost, baseline goals, scroll triggers, and initial audit report (firstReport) are permanently erased.
  • Local Application State & Permissions: Local preferences, enabled networks, custom filter overrides, app blocker configurations, and device permission states are wiped.
  • Usage & Shielding Statistics: Synchronized and local habit telemetry (including short videos hidden, feeds blocked, ads blocked, and focus time saved) are purged completely.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device registration tokens are deleted so you will never receive further alerts or updates.

3. Data Retention Timeline

Deletion is initiated immediately for your Hublu identity, Firestore profile, local settings, statistics, and browser sessions. Service-provider security, fraud-prevention, and operational logs may be retained for limited periods under the provider's legal and security requirements.

4. Manual Deletion Requests

If you have uninstalled the app or are unable to access your device, you can request a manual deletion of your account records by emailing our data support team from the email address associated with your Hublu account.
